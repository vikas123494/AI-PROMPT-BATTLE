# FoodBridge — Design and build prompt

Use this as the creative brief for iterating on the FoodBridge experience with an AI design or coding assistant.

> Act as a senior product designer and frontend engineer. Design and build a polished, responsive, one-page homepage for **FoodBridge**, a social-impact platform connecting restaurants, hotels, events and households with surplus food to verified NGOs and communities that can use it.
>
> **Core brand idea:** “Good food deserves another table.” The experience should feel like a thoughtful, premium social-impact startup—not a generic charity template. Lead with emotional clarity, trust and a simple action. Within the first five seconds, visitors should understand the food-waste problem, the human need and the role FoodBridge plays in connecting the two.
>
> **Visual direction:** warm editorial design; forest-green identity; warm cream canvas; restrained citrus-orange accents; oversized expressive typography; generous whitespace; documentary-style food and community photography; tactile image crops; small editorial labels; clean iconography. Use Manrope for display headings and DM Sans for body copy.
>
> **Information architecture:** announcement strip, responsive navigation, hero with donor CTA, problem statement, FoodBridge solution, four-step donation journey, feature list, impact storytelling, community stories, participation CTA, FAQ accordion and detailed footer.
>
> **Interaction requirements:** smooth in-page navigation, responsive mobile menu, animated section entrances, hover feedback, accordion FAQ, and accessible modal forms for food donors, volunteers and partners. Use required-field validation and a clear demo confirmation state. Never imply that a request was sent to a real operations team unless a backend is connected.
>
> **Trust and integrity:** do not fabricate impact statistics, partner logos, endorsements or testimonials. Use clearly labelled illustrative community stories and explain that verified impact figures will be populated as real donations are recorded. Make the website accessible, responsive and fast. Use semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful image alt text and reduced-motion support.
>
> **Stack:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion and Lucide React. Keep the implementation component-driven and deployment-ready for Vercel or Render.
>
> **Quality bar:** the final result should look like a credible early-stage product with a distinct visual identity, clear hierarchy, polished spacing, responsive layouts and purposeful micro-interactions. Prioritise the quality of the first impression and the clarity of the donation journey over adding unnecessary features.
